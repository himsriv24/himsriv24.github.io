Published online 02 April 2026. [DOI: 10.1007/s10439-026-04097-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10439-026-04097-7)

This paper presents a semi-supervised framework for automated three-phase seizure segmentation in SEEG. Seven envelope-based features (RMS, relative bandpower in θ/α/β/γ, line length, spectral entropy) are extracted and fed into the PELT changepoint algorithm with RBF kernel. Evaluated on 179 SOZ bipolar channels across 32 seizures from 10 Engel Class I patients. Achieves onset MAE of 4.19 ± 2.69 s (71.6% within 5 s) and termination MAE of 3.82 ± 4.24 s (75.0% within 5 s).
