A comprehensive introduction to data science principles and practices, covering data collection, cleaning, analysis, and visualization. Students gain practical skills for working with real-world datasets.

**Topics covered:** Data collection and APIs, data cleaning and preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, and data visualization.

**Prerequisites:** Basic Python programming and introductory statistics.
